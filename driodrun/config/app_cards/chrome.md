# Chrome Browser Guide

## First Launch
- May show "Use without an account" — tap it
- May show "Got it" or "Stay signed out" — tap to dismiss
- May show "No thanks" for sign-in prompts — dismiss

## Navigation
- Address bar at the top for entering URLs or search queries
- Three-dot menu (top-right) for settings and history
- Tabs button (top-right) to switch between tabs

## Search
- Tap the address bar and type search query
- Press Enter to search via Google
- Results page shows ads/sponsored at top — scroll past them
- Organic results appear below ads — look for the correct domain

## Identifying Sponsored vs Organic Results
- Sponsored results have "Sponsored" or "Ad" label
- Organic results show the actual website domain without ad labels
- Always click organic results, never sponsored

## Common Actions
- New Tab: Tap tabs button then Plus icon
- Close Tab: Swipe tab away in tab switcher
- Refresh: Pull down from the top of the page
- Back: Use Android back button or left arrow

## Dismissing Popups and Overlays
- NEVER press the Android BACK button to dismiss web page popups — it navigates away from the page.
- "Get Comet for your phone" / "Install Comet": tap OUTSIDE the popup on a dark/dimmed area to dismiss.
- "Open in App": IGNORE it completely. Do NOT tap it. Just scroll past it to find the input field.
- "Try app" / "Chat with Gemini in a app": IGNORE it. Scroll down to the input field.
- "Sign up" / "Log in": IGNORE it. Find the input field and type directly.
- For Chrome's own dialogs ("Use without an account", "Got it", "Stay signed out", "No thanks") — tap the text to dismiss. These are safe to tap.
- If the page asks to sign in, look for "Skip" or dismiss options.

## Reading AI Responses
- After an AI response loads, scroll slowly like a human reading.
- Scroll a small amount, wait 2 seconds, scroll again.
- Continue until you reach the very bottom of the response.
- Do not stop scrolling halfway through the answer.

## CAPTCHA Handling
- If Chrome shows a CAPTCHA page ("Our systems have detected unusual traffic", "I'm not a robot", image verification) -- do NOT try to solve it.
- Press the Android HOME button, then open the default Browser app (not Chrome).
- Continue the task in that browser instead.

## Tips
- Wait for pages to fully load before interacting
- If stuck on any popup for more than 2 attempts, press Android BACK button
